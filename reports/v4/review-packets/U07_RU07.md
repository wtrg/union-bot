2. **Autonomous Milestone Progression (`U07-002`, `U07-003`)**:
   - `progressNextMilestone`: automatically selects next `PENDING` milestone according to DAG order and marks `IN_PROGRESS`.
   - `completeMilestone`: records execution evidence references (`evidenceRefs`) and transitions milestone to `COMPLETED`.
   - Automatically transitions goal to `COMPLETED` when all milestones are finished.
3. **Execution Controls (`U07-004`)**:
   - Supports `/pause`, `/resume`, and `/stop` controls.
   - Halts new milestone dispatches when paused (`ERR_WORKFLOW_PAUSED`).
4. **Crash Recovery & Dangling Goal Resolution (`U07-005`)**:
   - `recoverDanglingGoals`: automatically cleans expired in-memory leases on startup, detects goals stuck with `IN_PROGRESS` milestones without active worker leases, and resets them safely to `PENDING` for clean pickup.
